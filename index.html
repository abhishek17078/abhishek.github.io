M.Tech Thesis May 2025

High-Resolution Long-Term Root Zone Soil Moisture Dataset for India

Integrated physics-based hydrological modeling (H08) with random forest machine learning to engineer a high-resolution (5km) soil moisture dataset mapping 1981–2024. Significantly enhances structural monitoring of drought vulnerabilities and micro-agricultural resource mapping.

Quantitative Geomorphology May 2026

Landscape Evolution of a Dharwad Craton Terrain

Designed a robust, coupled landscape evolution model in Landlab simulating 100 kyr of geomorphic processes. Parsed SRTM DEM profiles using D8 flow routing mechanisms to gauge equilibrium profiles across stable cratonic terrains.

Seismology Frameworks April 2026

Induced Seismicity Analysis at Húsmúli, Iceland

Conducted statistical analyses of seismic responses during water and CO₂ injection cycles. Utilized Gutenberg-Richter equations and seismogenic index dynamics to model injection-driven pore-pressure patterns.
